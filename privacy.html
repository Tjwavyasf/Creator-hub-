Privacy Policy

Creator Hub · last updated November 15, 2026

What Creator Hub is

Creator Hub is a personal tool that lets a creator post to and view analytics for their own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts. It runs entirely in the creator's own web browser.

What data it uses

  • Profile information, follower counts, video and post lists and statistics, comments, and audience insights from the accounts you connect, read using each platform's official API with your permission.
  • Videos, photos, captions and thumbnails you choose to post.

Where it is stored

Sign-in tokens, settings and a small daily history of your follower and view totals are stored only in your browser's local storage on your device. Creator Hub has no database and does not send your data to the app's developer or to anyone else.

TikTok and Instagram sign-in use a small helper service that you deploy and control on your own Cloudflare account. It only exchanges sign-in codes for tokens and forwards your requests to TikTok and Instagram. It does not store anything.

Sharing

Your data is only sent to the platform it belongs to (Google/YouTube, TikTok, Meta), and only when you take an action such as loading the dashboard, replying to a comment or posting.

Removing your data

Tap Disconnect in the Accounts tab, or clear this site's data in your browser. You can also remove Creator Hub's access from each platform's account settings: Google Account → Security → Third-party access, TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions, and Facebook/Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites.
